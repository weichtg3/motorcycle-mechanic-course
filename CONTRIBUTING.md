# Contributing

Course lessons are Markdown. Keep these conventions:

- Preserve module/lesson numbering.
- Every lesson must contain reading, a bike comparison, a required video, a garage exercise and knowledge check.
- Do not add safety-critical specifications without a reliable source and citation.
- Prefer manufacturer documentation and respected motorcycle-education channels.
- Keep YouTube videos external; do not download/re-host them.
- If replacing a video, update its approximate runtime and the master TOC if the duration materially changes.
