# The four training motorcycles

## 1995 Harley-Davidson Sportster 883 Hugger

Our compact Harley training platform. It teaches air-cooled Evolution-engine fundamentals, carburetion, cable controls, primary/clutch/transmission relationships and belt final drive in a relatively accessible chassis.

## 1996 Harley-Davidson Electra Glide Ultra Classic (FLHTCU)

Our touring Harley platform. Period references identify the model with a **carbureted 1340 Evolution engine** and **belt final drive**. The fairing, luggage, accessory circuits and greater vehicle mass make it ideal for learning access planning, electrical diagnosis and heavier chassis/service considerations.

## 1998 Honda Shadow Aero 1100

Honda's 1998 launch information describes the Aero with an **1,099 cc liquid-cooled four-stroke V-twin**, **five-speed transmission**, and **shaft final drive**. It gives us liquid cooling, Honda/Japanese packaging and shaft-drive service as contrasts to the Harleys.

## 2025 Harley-Davidson Road Glide

Our modern Harley benchmark. Harley-Davidson specifies a **Milwaukee-Eight 117**, **Electronic Sequential Port Fuel Injection (ESPFI)**, **liquid-cooled cylinder heads**, a **six-speed** touring drivetrain, **belt final drive**, selectable ride modes, Rider Safety Enhancements and a **12.3-inch TFT display with Skyline OS**. It lets the course compare traditional mechanical diagnosis with modern sensor/module/software-aware diagnosis.

:::info Verify exact specifications
Model-year references are useful for orientation, but exact torque values, fluid capacities, wear limits, cable free play and service procedures should always be checked against the correct owner/service manual for the exact motorcycle.
:::

## Comparison at a glance

| Area | 1995 Sportster 883 Hugger | 1996 Ultra Classic | 1998 Shadow Aero 1100 | 2025 Road Glide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Engine family | Harley Evolution Sportster V-twin | Harley Evolution Big Twin | Honda V-twin | Harley Milwaukee-Eight 117 |
| Cooling | Air-cooled | Air-cooled | Liquid-cooled | Air/oil + liquid-cooled cylinder heads |
| Fuel delivery | Carbureted | Carbureted | Carbureted | ESPFI electronic fuel injection |
| Transmission | 5-speed | 5-speed | 5-speed | 6-speed |
| Final drive | Belt | Belt | Shaft | Belt |
| Teaching strength | Simple mechanical relationships | Touring complexity/accessories | Cooling + shaft-drive contrast | EFI + ABS/rider aids + modern electronics |
