# 18.4 — Complete Inspection: 2025 Road Glide

**Estimated time:** Reading 35m · Video ~12m · Garage 120m · **Total ~167m**

## Objective

Apply the entire course to a modern touring motorcycle without confusing electronic sophistication with permission to skip mechanical fundamentals. Build a baseline inspection that covers tires/wheels, brakes, suspension/steering, controls, leaks, fluids, cooling, battery/charging condition, lighting, belt final drive, engine behavior, warning indicators and rider-interface status.

## Modern Road Glide checkpoints

- Record tire condition/pressure and inspect wheel-speed-sensor areas without disturbing them.
- Inspect brake hardware and fluid condition; ABS service follows factory procedures.
- Inspect the final-drive belt and visible pulleys/guards using factory criteria.
- Check for fluid leaks and inspect the liquid-cooled-head radiator/cooling system only when cold.
- Establish battery condition and charging behavior before interpreting electronic symptoms.
- Start the motorcycle and note warning lamps/messages; do not erase diagnostic information merely to make a warning disappear.
- Exercise normal controls, lighting and TFT functions.
- Record ride-mode and rider-safety configuration rather than changing settings blindly.
- Note accessories or modifications that may affect wiring, calibration or diagnostics.

## Watch — required

[The 4-stroke engine, how does it work?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s8mEXQXqA7Q) — revisit the mechanical foundation before evaluating the electronic layers.

## Garage assignment

Create a one-page baseline report with four columns: **system**, **observation**, **test/spec needed**, and **next action**. Classify each next action as DIY inspection, DIY service with factory procedure, advanced diagnostic work, or professional/dealer-level work.

:::warning
Do not perform ABS bleeding, module programming, calibration, safety-system modification or specification-dependent disassembly without the correct 2025 factory procedure and required equipment.
:::
